# 1. Problem Statement & Business Need

## Problem Statement

CivicConnect currently manages community service requests through a fragmented mix of channels — email, telephone, WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets and paper records. Because no single controlled record exists, requests are frequently duplicated, overlooked, incorrectly assigned or lost between channels. Requesters have little visibility into whether a request has been received, assigned, delayed, resolved or closed, which erodes trust in the organisation's service delivery. Staff struggle to prioritise work, establish clear ownership of individual requests, and coordinate effort across the team, while accountability for status changes and actions taken is weak because there is no single traceable history per request. Management, in turn, lacks reliable, consolidated information on outstanding, overdue and resolved work, making reporting manual, inconsistent and difficult to audit.

## Business Need

CivicConnect needs a controlled digital platform that gives requesters, staff and management a single, reliable and traceable way to submit, manage, monitor and report on service requests — improving visibility and accountability across the request lifecycle without introducing an unsustainable technical, operational or financial burden on the organisation.
